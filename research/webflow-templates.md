# Webflow template shortlist – Orchidee (21 Nov 2026)

Every template below was checked against its marketplace page and live demo. Prices were taken while Webflow was running a "50% off until Dec 3" banner, so recheck them at checkout.

None of the templates has a sticky phone/callback bar, so that is a custom build on every option. The application form (name, phone, email, role, qualification, earliest start date) also has to be built on every option, then connected to HubSpot.

| # | Template | Creator | Price | Careers page | Why it fits / what changes |
|---|----------|---------|-------|--------------|----------------------------|
| 1 | [Ongoing Care 128](https://webflow.com/templates/html/ongoingcare128-health-website-template) · [demo](https://ongoing-care-128.webflow.io/) | 128.digital | $79 | Yes, with job-detail CMS | **Top pick.** Built for home care. Large body text, light cream base, one coral accent that can become #E6007E. Cut events, living options and the extra home/about layouts, and add the legal pages. |
| 2 | [Medic 128](https://webflow.com/templates/html/medic-health-website-template) · [demo](https://medic-128.webflow.io/) | 128.digital | $49 | Yes, with job pages and a form | Already has a pink accent, so recolouring is easy. Reads clinic rather than home care: swap the teal for violet and rewrite "departments" as Leistungen. |
| 3 | [Serenova](https://webflow.com/templates/html/serenova-website-template) · [demo](https://serenovas.webflow.io/) | Deenmik | $79 | Yes, no form | Senior home care with legal pages built in. Dark green and black sections need a full recolour. |
| 4 | [Elderhaven](https://webflow.com/templates/html/elderhaven-website-template) · [demo](https://elderhaven-wcopilot.webflow.io) | wcopilot | $79 | Yes, no form | Clean cream base with one orange accent. Has a lot of pages to strip back. |
| 5 | [Serenica](https://webflow.com/templates/html/serenica-website-template) · [demo](https://serenica.webflow.io/) | Alviansyah Razak | $49 | No | The most minimal and Apple-like of the six, made for home care. Careers and legal pages have to be built from scratch. |
| 6 | [Clinically](https://webflow.com/templates/html/clinically-doctor-website-template) · [demo](https://clinically-template.webflow.io) | Pixeflow | $49 | Static page only | Hard to reduce to one accent colour, and it feels like a clinic or startup. |

Two more were checked and dropped because neither has a careers page: ElderlyCare X (BRIX, $129) and Medura (Olynex, $59).

**Direction:** Ongoing Care 128, trimmed to Startseite, Leistungen, Über uns, Kontakt/Anfrage, Karriere and the legal pages. Recolour coral to Orchidee magenta, use violet sparingly as the secondary colour, enlarge the headlines and add a sticky mobile call/callback bar. If the client wants maximum white space, Serenica is the minimal alternative.
