Rebuild of orchidee-pflegedienst.de (ambulante Pflege, Koblenz, Fidelis Unternehmensgruppe). German site.
Brief: `brief/` (from Halyl, 2026-11-18). Logo: `assets/logo/`. Meeting notes/transcript: `meeting/`.

**Goal:** two conversions – new patients (Pflegegrad 3–5 with Behandlungspflege) and new nurses (PDL, Pflegefachkraft).
**CTAs:** call · callback · Anfrage form · Bewerbung form → all into HubSpot, tracked.
**Pages:** Startseite · Leistungen · Über uns · Kontakt/Anfrage · Karriere · Impressum · Datenschutz
**Style:** simple, Apple-like white space with impact; white/neutral base, logo magenta as the single accent, violet sparingly.
**Open:** HubSpot routing, tracking IDs (Meta pixel, analytics), hosting/domain access.
